Phase 01

The Source.

Phase 02

Time is an
Ingredient.

The Ancestral Method

Hand-Churned. Wood-Fired. Authentic.

Step 01

A2 Milk Collection

Before dawn breaks, our indigenous Gir cows are hand-milked. We strictly follow the 'Dohan' system, ensuring the calf drinks its share first. This cruelty-free milk is highly nutritious and rich in A2 beta-casein protein.

🥛
🫙

Step 02

Cultured into Curd

Unlike industrial ghee made directly from raw cream, we follow the authentic Ayurvedic tradition. The fresh milk is gently boiled over a wood-fire and inoculated with a natural starter culture, transforming it into probiotic-rich curd (Dahi) overnight.

Step 03

The Wooden Bilona Churn

During the ambrosial hours of Brahma Muhurta (before sunrise), the curd is hand-churned in both directions using a traditional wooden bilona. This bi-directional movement separates the rich, creamy Makhan (butter).

🪵
🔥

Step 04

Slow-Flame Cooking

The pure Makhan is carefully separated and transferred to a brass vessel. It is slow-cooked over a mild cow-dung fire. This slow clarification removes all milk solids and moisture, leaving behind a crystalline, golden elixir.

Step 05

Pure A2 Ghee

The result is 100% pure, granular, and aromatic A2 Gir Cow Bilona Ghee. Hand-filtered and packaged in glass jars to retain its therapeutic properties and exceptional flavour.
