The Ancestral Process

Uncompromising
Purity.

Phase 01

The Source.

It begins at the source. Pure A2 milk from indigenous, grass-fed Gir cows. No industrial shortcuts, no crowded feedlots — just a foundation of absolute, unyielding quality on our own farm.

Phase 02

Time is an
Ingredient.

We do not rush nature. The milk is slowly cultured into rich, earthen dahi overnight in traditional clay pots. Patience transforms the raw into the profound.

Phase 03

The Rhythm of
Tradition.

Bidirectional. Methodical. The traditional wooden Bilona churns the curd before dawn, extracting the pristine Makhan while the buttermilk falls away. Hand-churned, just as it was centuries ago.

Phase 04

Forged in
Warmth.

Slow-simmered over a gentle, open flame. As the heat rises, the liquid clarifies, revealing a golden, nutrient-dense nectar with an unmistakable, roasted aroma.
